# Brand foundation

- Name: Zyberlane; display: ZYBERLANE.
- Descriptor: TECHNOLOGY & TRANSFORMATION COMPANY.
- Tagline: Built Beyond Boundaries.
- Supporting line: From Brand Identity to Physical Intelligence.
- Domain: zyberlane.com.
- Positioning: Technology & transformation brand, not a claim of incorporation.
- Voice: precise, clear, ambitious, factual. Avoid invented clients, reviews, outcomes, registrations or claims that future engineering capabilities are already offered.
- Architecture: one parent mark and wordmark; approved product suffixes / XR and / LABS. Products retain their functional design and permission boundaries.
- Social: use the centered 54%-width avatar; do not crop or zoom. Publish only verified accounts. GitHub and WhatsApp links in the source are not proof of ownership; unverified Instagram, Threads, X and Facebook handles must not be invented.
- Public use: use unmodified assets for accurate references to Zyberlane; no misleading endorsement or ownership claims. Third-party fonts retain their respective licenses.
